# Sources and verification scope

Checked October 10, 2026. Entry-level URLs are stored in `src/data/library/` and are the authoritative source mapping. Descriptions, household stories, background histories, and CAS combinations are original Townieverse writing. Names and basic relationships between game fields are researched facts.

## Official game records

- EA storefront catalogs: [expansions](https://www.ea.com/games/the-sims/the-sims-4/store/categories/expansion-packs), [game packs](https://www.ea.com/games/the-sims/the-sims-4/store/categories/game-packs), [stuff packs](https://www.ea.com/games/the-sims/the-sims-4/store/categories/stuff-packs), [kits](https://www.ea.com/games/the-sims/the-sims-4/store/categories/kits), [kit page 2](https://www.ea.com/games/the-sims/the-sims-4/store/categories/kits/2), [kit page 3](https://www.ea.com/games/the-sims/the-sims-4/store/categories/kits/3). The omitted Maker capsule and Digital Deluxe item are outside this application's pack types. Country Kitchen is additionally checked against [its EA product page](https://www.ea.com/da/games/the-sims/the-sims-4/buy/addon/the-sims-4-country-kitchen-kit).
- EA announcements: [Life & Death](https://www.ea.com/games/the-sims/the-sims-4/news/life-and-death-expansion-pack), [Nordhaven creator builds](https://www.ea.com/games/the-sims/the-sims-4/news/the-sims-4-businesses-and-hobbies-creator-builds), [Enchanted by Nature](https://www.ea.com/games/the-sims/the-sims-4/news/enchanted-by-nature-expansion-pack), [Adventure Awaits](https://www.ea.com/games/the-sims/the-sims-4/news/adventure-awaits-expansion-pack), [Royalty & Legacy](https://www.ea.com/games/the-sims/the-sims-4/news/royalty-and-legacy-expansion-pack). These primary sources support the latest world/pack and neighborhood names.
- Earlier world/pack mapping cross-checks: Wikipedia's [expansion table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sims_4_expansion_packs), [game-pack table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sims_4_game_packs), and the Sims Wiki's [World](https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/World) listing. These are secondary community/reference sources, **not EA-authored data**. Indexed wiki text was available where direct page loading was restricted.
- Exact lot facts: Sims Wiki [Foundry Cove](https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/Foundry_Cove) and [Gallery/list of lots](https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/The_Sims_4_Gallery/List_of_lots). The four included lots have checked dimensions and neighborhood membership. No assumptions about prices, original household occupancy, or revised interiors are carried into the catalog.
- Careers: [EA's career index](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/the-sims/the-sims-4/the-sims-4-careers/) and linked individual career pages. Each included career stores its individual page, which states its base-game/pack dependency. Restrictions are conservative where life-stage coverage is not fully documented. New careers not yet individually modeled remain excluded.
- Traits and three adult/teen aspirations: [EA's original player guide](https://cdn-assets-ts4.pulse.ea.com/Guide/TheSims4_Players_Guide.pdf), with [Sims Wiki trait-conflict reference](https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/Trait_conflict) for the no-mutual-conflict subset. The old guide verifies the included base-game names; it is **not** evidence of a complete current trait/aspiration catalog.
- Childhood aspirations: [EA child-skills guide](https://help.ea.com/no/articles/the-sims/the-sims-4/child-skills/). Only the four base-game aspirations are included from this page.

## Fashion terminology

Sources verify that the names are used; they do not turn authored CAS tips into mandatory rules or establish universal definitions.

- Museum sources: [V&A on punk and New Romantic](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/vivienne-westwood-punk-new-romantic-and-beyond), [London Museum on Teddy Boys](https://www.londonmuseum.org.uk/collections/london-stories/teddy-boys-britains-first-youth-subculture/), [Museum at FIT on hip-hop style](https://www.fitnyc.edu/museum/exhibitions/hip-hop-style/).
- Community/editorial sources: [The COMM on Dolly/Cult Party/Mori](https://the-comm.online/blog/the-history-of-girly-styles-dolly-kei-cult-party-kei-and-mori-girl-part-one/), [FUN! JAPAN's gyaru guide](https://www.fun-japan.jp/en/articles/12779), [Dazed on Kinderwhore](https://www.dazeddigital.com/fashion/article/62252/1/90s-kinderwhore-girlcore-girl-trends-courtney-love-hole-batsheva-celine), [Who What Wear on Whimsigoth](https://www.whowhatwear.com/whimsigoth-aesthetic), [GQ on dark/light academia](https://www.gq.com/story/dark-academia-vs-light-academia-aesthetics-style-explainer), Vogue's [Balletcore](https://www.vogue.com/article/balletcore-a-look-back-at-how-designers-have-been-inspired-by-dance), [Coquette](https://www.vogue.com/article/coquette-aesthetic-explainer), and [aesthetic overview](https://www.vogue.com/article/core-aesthetic-microtrends-2023), plus [Dictionary.com's Gorpcore entry](https://www.dictionary.com/culture/fashion/gorpcore).
- Secondary/community references: Wikipedia pages for Japanese street fashion, Rivethead, Psychobilly, Goblincore, Mod, Greaser, Grunge, Emo, Steampunk fashion, and Indie Sleaze; Japanese Fashion Wikia's Dark Mori entry. These support terminology, not a claim that all styles originated in Japan or that all histories are uncontested.
- Commercial style/editorial references are explicitly narrower evidence: FUGA's gothic-style guide, The Punk Rock Princess's gothic-western article, and Printful's cyberpunk-fashion guide. Frutiger Aero is checked against a dedicated community design-aesthetic reference; its clothing interpretation is Townieverse's own.
- Forty everyday descriptive guides need no claimed historical origin and are marked verification-not-applicable. Twelve legacy examples are explicitly authored rather than relabeled as established subcultures. Aliases stay inside their canonical entries.

Future additions should use primary museums, publications, community first-hand documentation, or official EA pages where possible. Record actual scope, source date, and uncertainty; avoid copying historical claims from a broad list as if they had been individually confirmed.
